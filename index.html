Case files

Verified

Closing the traceability gap

Challenge

No structured way to trace quality issues to their source. Recurring problems kept resurfacing.

Approach

Structured DMAIC project: root-caused across process and communication behaviour, ran targeted interventions.

Outcome

Significant, sustained improvement in traceability compliance, verified with real data.

Verified

Pre-production quality gate

Challenge

Design files reached the floor with no automated check. Errors caught only after material and time were spent.

Approach

Built a digital QA system validating files before they reach a machine, catching structural and sizing issues automatically.

Outcome

Physical test-cutting eliminated for validated files; defects caught at the design stage.

Verified

Fleet-wide file distribution & audit

Challenge

Auditing files across a multi-machine fleet was manual, slow, and at scale, impractical to run at all.

Approach

Automated distribution on a fixed schedule with a full, timestamped audit trail. Zero manual steps.

Outcome

A process that was previously impractical to run now takes about half an hour, fully traceable.

Verified

Predictive production planning

Challenge

Time estimates relied on manual test-runs and rough averages. Slow, and didn't scale.

Approach

Built a predictive model calibrated on real historical production data, capturing the factors that actually drive time.

Outcome

Faster, more consistent estimates feeding directly into planning and scheduling.

Verified

Standardising machine files

Challenge

Every new product range needed hours of manual file preparation before anything could run.

Approach

Replaced one-off files with a standard set and built-in alignment checks.

Outcome

The prep step disappeared completely. Time to test a new range down 93%.

Verified

Smarter picking

Approach

A tool that maps efficient routes and sequences orders to match.

Outcome

Questions

Straight answers

Do I need to know what the problem is?

No. Most of what I've fixed wasn't on anyone's list. Tell me what feels slow or keeps going wrong, and I'll find where the time goes.

Will it work with our ERP?

Yes. I work from the exports and reports your system already produces, whether that's Sage, Epicor or something else. Nothing inside your ERP changes, so upgrades and support stay exactly as they are.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The tool, the code and the documentation. No licence fees, no subscription.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere. Tools run on your own machines and most work fully offline. I don't keep copies of your data once the job is done.

What if something breaks later?

The first 30 days of fixes are included. After that, you ask for changes only when you need them, priced before any work starts. No retainer.

How long does it take?

Small tools usually take one to two weeks, larger ones three to six. You get a date with the quote.
