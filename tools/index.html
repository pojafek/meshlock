Around the ERP

Job travellers, sorted in a minute

Challenge

Job travellers came off the ERP as one long, unsorted print. Someone spent about an hour a day sorting multi-page orders by hand.

Approach

A tool that sorts sheets by priority and order, adds a separator with the sheet count, stamps the courier and prints a preview of the part from its cut code.

Outcome

About an hour of daily sorting now takes about a minute.

1 h → 1 min

Quality & error-proofing

Pre-production quality gate

Challenge

Design files reached the floor with no automated check. Errors caught only after material and time were spent.

Approach

A digital check on every cut file before it reaches a machine: orientation, position on the bed, open contours, piece count against the paperwork, and a predicted cut time.

Outcome

Physical test-cutting eliminated for validated files. Defects caught at the design stage, not by a customer.

Up to 10% of machine capacity freed

Data & reporting

Machine run data and the cut time model

Challenge

Machine logs held years of run data nobody could read. Time estimates relied on manual test-runs and rough averages.

Approach

Three years of raw machine logs cleaned and matched to jobs, with every start and stop tracked to the second. A predictive model built on that history estimates cut time for new designs.

Outcome

Every stop is visible and can be followed up. Faster, more consistent estimates feed straight into planning.

3 years of logs, now live data
